OpenCV Pipeline
1. Grayscale Conversion Discards chrominance channels; reduces data size by 66%
2. Contrast Enhancement (CLAHE) Adaptive histogram equalization to bring out faint ink
3. Gaussian Denoising Suppresses high-frequency camera and scanner grain
4. Adaptive Thresholding Local binarization; ideal for shadows and gradients
5. Otsu Thresholding Optimal global binarization minimizing intra-class variance
6. Bicubic Upscaling Enlarges small fonts to improve OCR character detection

Educational Guidance: Image Preprocessing

Raw photos often have shadows, camera noise, or uneven contrast that degrade OCR accuracy. Applying grayscale conversion and adaptive thresholding creates crisp bi-level text, giving Tesseract clean character boundaries.
